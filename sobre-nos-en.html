Formats change. Passion remains.

As CDs became popular in the 1990s, vinyl lost prominence and many collections were put away. Interest later grew again. Today, collections bring together familiar albums, unexpected discoveries and less common editions. Not every record is rare, but each can mean something special to the person seeking it.

Starting a vinyl collection.

Start with albums you want to listen to. Explore record shops, second-hand shops, fairs and online platforms. Talk to collectors, compare copies and check condition and terms before buying or trading. A collection reflects your choices, not just price or rarity.

Storing and organising records.

Choose stable shelving suited to the weight and size of your collection. Store records upright, supported and without excessive pressure. Avoid horizontal piles. Group similar formats and use dividers to make browsing easier and keep records properly supported.
