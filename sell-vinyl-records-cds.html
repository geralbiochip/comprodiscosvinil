Selling guide

How to sell vinyl records and CDs

To sell vinyl records or CDs in Cascais, Parede, Carcavelos, Oeiras or Greater Lisbon, start by sharing your location and photos. Assessment and any visit are arranged directly with Paulo Morais.

Prepare your collection

Give an approximate number of LPs, singles and CDs and your location. You do not need to identify every edition before getting in touch.

Photograph the records

Send an overview, front and back covers and readable labels. Show scratches or missing items. Avoid personal documents and faces.

How is an offer made?

A possible purchase depends on titles, editions, condition and interest for Paulo Morais’s personal collection. Details may need checking before agreeing on a price.

Confirm payment and delivery

Before shipping, confirm the items, price, payment, costs and delivery. An enquiry does not commit you to selling or guarantee a purchase.
